**How do I reset my password?**
Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page; the link is valid for 30 minutes.

**Where is my order?**
Orders ship within 2 business days. The tracking link is in the confirmation email.

**Can I change my plan?**
Yes, any time from Settings → Billing. Changes apply from the next billing cycle.

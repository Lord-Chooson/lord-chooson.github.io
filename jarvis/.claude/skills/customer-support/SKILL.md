# Customer support

1. Read `faqs.md` and `refund-policy.md` in this skill folder.
2. Draft a reply using `templates/reply.md`, in my tone (see `me.md`).
3. If the question is not covered by the FAQ or policy, do not guess: say "ESCALATE" and explain
   what is missing.
4. Never invent policy, prices, dates or promises. Always draft — never send.
